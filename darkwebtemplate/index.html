Caution

The dark web is a part of the internet that's intentionally hidden and requires special software like Tor to access. While it has some legitimate uses, it's overwhelmingly associated with illegal and dangerous activities.
Do not attempt to access the dark web unless you are a trained cybersecurity professional with a specific and legitimate reason.
The anonymity offered by the dark web attracts those seeking to operate outside the law. Law enforcement struggles to monitor activity, making it a haven for illegal marketplaces, hacking forums, and extremist groups. Even accidental exposure can have severe consequences.

WHY THE WARNING

The dark web is a part of the internet that is not indexed by traditional search engines. It requires specific software, configurations, or authorization to access. While it can be used for privacy-focused purposes, it is often associated with illegal activities and dangerous content.

How To Stay Safe ?

Unless you're a trained cybersecurity professional, accessing the dark web is strongly discouraged.
